Show one local mxv workspace with its checkouts and units

Usage: flightdeck workspaces show [OPTIONS] <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>  The workspace's name, as `flightdeck workspaces list` prints it

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flightdeck workspaces show reading-list --json

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --no-input. `flightdeck workspaces show --help` describes them.
