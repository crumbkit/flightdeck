List the workflow graphs with their node counts and whether each is drawn

Usage: flightdeck workflows list [OPTIONS]

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flightdeck workflows list
  flightdeck workflows list --json --full

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flightdeck workflows list --help` describes them.
