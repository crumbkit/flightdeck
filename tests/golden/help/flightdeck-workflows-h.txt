Read the workflow graphs of a board

Usage: flightdeck workflows [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  list  List the workflow graphs with their node counts and whether each is drawn
  show  Show one workflow graph with its problems, live records and freshness

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flightdeck workflows --help` describes them.
Next: flightdeck workflows <COMMAND> -h
