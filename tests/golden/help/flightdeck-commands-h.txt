Explore flightdeck's commands

Usage: flightdeck commands [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  list  List a branch of flightdeck's commands with their arguments, examples and columns

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flightdeck commands --help` describes them.
