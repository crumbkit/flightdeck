Explore the environment variables that flightdeck reads

Usage: flightdeck self environment [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  list  List the variables that flightdeck and crumbkit read, with their rules and values

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Global flags: --json, --fields <FIELD>, --long, --full, --no-input. `flightdeck self environment --help` describes them.
