Show one workflow graph with its problems, live records and freshness

Usage: flightdeck workflows show [OPTIONS] <SLUG>

Arguments:
  <SLUG>  The graph's slug, as `flightdeck workflows list` prints it

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Examples:
  flightdeck workflows show development-flow --json

Effects: read-only, closed-world.
Global flags: --json, --no-input. `flightdeck workflows show --help` describes them.
